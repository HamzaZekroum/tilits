ABOUT

Til Its Cover, Lenny Kravitz Tribute Band

ROCK · FUNK · SOUL · R&B

Six musicians onstage. Two hours live. All the energy of Lenny Kravitz.

The opening riff of Are You Gonna Go My Way. The groove of Fly Away. The vocals front and center on I Belong to You. With Zak on vocals and guitar, Til Its Cover brings Lenny Kravitz’s signature songs to the stage in a set that moves between electric rock, soul, funk, and stripped-back acoustic moments.

From Let Love Rule to Fly Away, the band leans into the contrasts that give the songs their character. The guitars hit hard, the grooves get the room moving, and the quieter songs draw the crowd in.
